Answers:

- **A. lr = 5e-4 vs U0.** It has the highest mean val R@20 (.1307) and N@20 (.0724). The gain over U0
  s0 is +.0055 R@20 and is spread over all 4 tasks (each +.004 to +.006; CI excludes 0 on both
  long-view tasks, includes 0 on both like tasks). It is numerically above the U0 seed max (.1252) and is
  +.0087 above the 3-seed mean, i.e. about 1.2x the observed seed range (.0072). With one seed of the
  candidate and three of the baseline, this is **a promising but not a demonstrated improvement**.
- **B. tau.** tau = 0.03 and 0.1 are both within noise of U0 on the primary metric (-.0007 / -.0005).
  tau = 0.1 reallocates performance: long-view +.0085 / +.0094 (CIs exclude 0), likes -.0114 / -.0083
  (video-like CI excludes 0). tau = 0.03 changes nothing noticeable. Neither is better than U0 on the
  pre-registered objective; tau = 0.1 is a clear long-view vs like trade-off, not a degradation
  overall.
- **C. Task concentration.** lr5e4: no trade-off (all four tasks up). tau010: strongly concentrated
  (long-view up, likes down); a "video long-view improvement" alone would misrepresent it.
  tau003: flat everywhere.
- **D. Substantive conclusions supportable now.** Only descriptive ones: (i) training-seed variation of
  the 4-task mean val R@20 is about ±.004 (range .0072), larger than the user-bootstrap SE (~.0015-.002),
  so single-seed differences below ~.007 are not interpretable; (ii) the temperature in [0.03, 0.1]
  shifts the long-view/like balance rather than the overall level; (iii) a lower lr is a candidate
  improvement that needs replication. Nothing here supports replacing the formal U0.
- **E. More tuning?** Not broad tuning. The highest of three candidate scores carries a selection
  (winner's-curse) bias, and 3 of 7 D1-10 configs are used. The informative next step is replication
  of the one promising candidate (x_lr5e4 seeds 1, 2) plus a same-SHA control, not new values.

## 5. Should `x_u0_repro` be run? (not submitted)

`x_u0_repro` = U0 at the explore SHA 1051f86, seed 0, lr 1e-3, tau 0.05 (only `exp` / `run_root`
differ from the formal config).

- Value: removes the SHA / run confound from every seed-0 comparison. If it reproduces job 27's
  `val_history` bitwise (tier A), the formal U0 is a valid same-code control and the s1/s2 spread is
  pure seed variation; if not, all Δ vs U0 s0 must be re-based on x_u0_repro. It is also the
  prerequisite regression run planned for Wave 2.
- Risk: none to formal artifacts (separate clone / RUN_ROOT). CUDA kernels may not be bitwise
  reproducible across jobs even with deterministic settings; then tier B tolerances apply (plan §3.4).
  It needs a new config (`configs/explore/x_u0_repro.yaml`) → a new explore commit + push before
  submission (the running clone must not be edited in place).
- Cost: ~23 min on one A100 (+ ~5 min setup); it can run in parallel with x_lr5e4 s1/s2 (~25 min each).

Recommended (pending approval): one wave of three jobs in parallel: `x_u0_repro`, `x_lr5e4_s1`,
`x_lr5e4_s2` (~30 min wall clock), then a 3-vs-3 seed comparison (U0 {repro, s1, s2} vs lr5e4
